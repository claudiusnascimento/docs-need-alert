Google gives your team a full suite of productivity tools, all accessible from one place. Here's a quick look at what's available.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Task Management" icon="circle-check">
    Create, assign, and track tasks with due dates, priorities, and custom statuses so your team always knows what needs to be done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collaboration" icon="users">
    Comment on tasks, mention teammates, share files, and work together in real time — no matter where your team is located.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    Connect Google to the tools you already use, including calendars, communication apps, and file storage services.

  </Card>
