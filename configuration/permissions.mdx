Google uses a role-based permission system to give you precise control over what each person in your workspace can see and do. Every user you invite receives one of four roles — Owner, Admin, Member, or Viewer — and each role carries a distinct set of capabilities. Keeping permissions well-organized protects sensitive project data, prevents accidental changes to workspace settings, and ensures the right people have the right level of access at every stage of your work.

## Role Overview

Use the table below as a quick reference for what each role can and cannot do across your workspace.

| Role | Can View | Can Edit Tasks | Can Manage Members | Can Change Settings | Can Delete Workspace |
|------|:--------:|:--------------:|:------------------:|:-------------------:|:--------------------:|
| **Owner** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Admin** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| **Member** | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| **Viewer** | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |

<Accordion title="Role Descriptions">
  **Owner** — Full, unrestricted access to every feature, setting, and destructive action in the workspace. Only the Owner can permanently delete the workspace or transfer ownership to another user. Each workspace has exactly one Owner at a time.

  **Admin** — Broad administrative access including the ability to manage members, configure workspace settings, and customize labels and statuses. Admins cannot delete the workspace itself.

  **Member** — The standard role for active contributors. Members can create, edit, assign, comment on, and complete tasks, but cannot change workspace-level settings or manage other users.

  **Viewer** — Read-only access. Viewers can browse projects and tasks and leave comments but cannot create or modify any task content. Ideal for stakeholders or clients who need visibility without editing rights.
</Accordion>

## Assigning Roles

You can set a user's role at the point of invitation or update it at any time from the Members settings page.

### Invite a New User

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Member Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Members** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click the **+ Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Members page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Email Address">
    Type the invitee's email address in the **Email** field. You can add multiple addresses separated by commas to send bulk invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Role">
    Use the **Role** dropdown to assign **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**. The role takes effect as soon as the invitee accepts the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with a secure link to join your workspace. Pending invitations are listed under **Settings → Members → Pending Invites** and can be revoked at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change an Existing User's Role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Member Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Members** and locate the user in the active members list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Role Menu">
    Click the current role badge displayed next to the user's name to open the role selector dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the New Role">
    Choose the desired role from the dropdown. The change takes effect immediately — the user's permissions update without requiring them to log out or reload.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Project-Level Permissions

In addition to workspace-wide roles, you can restrict individual projects so that only specific members can access them. This is useful for confidential work, client-specific projects, or early-stage initiatives not ready for the full team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Project Settings">
    Navigate to the project you want to restrict. Click the **⋯** menu in the project header and select **Project Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Project Visibility">
    Under **Visibility**, switch from **All Workspace Members** to **Specific Members**. The project immediately becomes invisible to anyone not on the allowlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Members to the Project">
    Click **+ Add Member** and search for the users or teams you want to grant access. Each added member inherits their workspace role within the project — a workspace Member cannot gain Admin abilities at the project level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove Project Access">
    To revoke a user's access, click the **✕** next to their name in the project members list. They immediately lose visibility of the project and all its tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Workspace Owners and Admins always retain access to all projects regardless of project-level visibility settings. This ensures Admins can audit and manage every part of the workspace without needing to be manually added to each project.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Every workspace must have exactly one Owner at all times. Before transferring the Owner role to another user, confirm that the new Owner has accepted their invitation and has an active account. If you remove or demote the sole Owner without first designating a replacement, you will lose the ability to delete the workspace, manage billing, or perform other Owner-exclusive actions. To transfer ownership, go to **Settings → Members**, click **⋯** next to the new Owner's name, and select **Transfer Ownership**.
</Warning>
