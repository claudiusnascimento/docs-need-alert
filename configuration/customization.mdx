Google is designed to adapt to the way your team actually works, not the other way around. Through the customization options available in **Settings → Customize**, you can define your own labels, build task statuses that reflect your real workflow stages, save reusable project templates, and adjust the visual display to suit your preferences. Explore each section below to tailor your workspace from the ground up.

<Note>
  Some customizations — such as custom labels, task statuses, and project templates — are **workspace-level** and apply to every member. Others — such as theme, accent color, and compact view — are **personal preferences** stored per user account and do not affect how teammates see the workspace.
</Note>

## Custom Labels

Labels let you categorize and filter tasks across every project in your workspace. You can create as many labels as your team needs and assign a distinct color to each one for quick visual scanning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Labels Manager">
    Navigate to **Settings → Customize → Labels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Label">
    Click **+ New Label**, enter a name (e.g., `Bug`, `High Priority`, `Blocked`), and press **Enter** or click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a Color">
    Click the color swatch next to any label to open the color picker. Choose from the preset palette or enter a custom hex code to match your team's color conventions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Labels to Tasks">
    Open any task, click the **Labels** field in the task detail panel, and select one or more labels from the dropdown. Labels appear as color-coded chips on the task card in board and list views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder or Delete Labels">
    Drag labels by their handle to reorder them in the picker dropdown. To delete a label, click the **⋯** menu beside it and select **Delete**. Deleting a label removes it from all tasks it was applied to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom Task Statuses

By default, tasks move through **To Do → In Progress → Done**. If your team has more nuanced stages — such as review, QA, or blocked states — you can create a fully custom status pipeline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Status Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Customize → Task Statuses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a New Status">
    Click **+ Add Status**, type the status name (e.g., `In Review`, `QA Testing`, `Awaiting Feedback`), and select a status type: **Active**, **Done**, or **Cancelled**. The type determines how tasks in this status are counted in progress metrics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename an Existing Status">
    Click the pencil icon next to any status name to edit it inline. Press **Enter** to confirm the rename.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder Statuses">
    Drag statuses up or down to set the order in which they appear in the status picker and on board columns. The sequence reflects your team's natural progression.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Default Status">
    Click **Set as Default** on the status that should be pre-selected when a new task is created. Only one status can be the default at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Project Templates

When your team repeats similar project structures — such as onboarding checklists, sprint setups, or content calendars — save time by converting an existing project into a reusable template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save a Project as a Template">
    Open the project you want to template. Click the **⋯** menu in the project header and select **Save as Template**. Give the template a descriptive name and choose whether to include existing tasks, assignees, and due date offsets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Your Templates">
    View all saved templates under **Settings → Customize → Project Templates**. From here you can rename, preview, or delete any template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Project from a Template">
    When creating a new project, click **Use a Template** instead of starting blank. Select the template from the list, set a project start date, and Google automatically adjusts all relative due dates based on that anchor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before saving a template, remove or anonymize any sensitive task details — such as client names or confidential notes — since templates are visible to all workspace Admins and Owners.
</Tip>

## Themes & Display

Adjust how Google looks on your screen without changing anything for other team members. These settings are stored per user account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Light / Dark Mode">
    Go to your **Profile → Display Preferences** and toggle between **Light**, **Dark**, and **System Default** modes. System Default automatically follows your operating system's appearance setting.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Accent Color">
    Under **Display Preferences → Accent Color**, choose from the preset swatches or enter a custom hex value. The accent color is applied to buttons, active states, and progress indicators throughout the interface.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Compact View">
    Enable **Compact View** to reduce the vertical padding on task list rows, allowing you to see more tasks on screen at once without scrolling. Toggle it off to return to the default comfortable spacing.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
