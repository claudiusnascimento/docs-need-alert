The Settings dashboard is your central control panel for everything that defines how your Google workspace looks, behaves, and operates. Whether you need to update your workspace identity, adjust your billing plan, fine-tune default task behavior, or review your data policies, every configuration option lives here. Navigate to **Settings** from the left sidebar to get started.

## General Settings

Your general settings control the core identity of your workspace — the details that teammates and guests see when they interact with your environment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General Settings">
    Go to **Settings → General** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Workspace Name">
    Enter a new name in the **Workspace Name** field. This name appears in the top navigation bar and in all email notifications sent to your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Workspace Slug">
    Update the **Workspace Slug** to change your unique URL (e.g., `app.google.com/your-slug`). Slugs must be lowercase, contain no spaces, and use hyphens as separators. Changing your slug immediately invalidates any previously shared workspace links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a Logo">
    Click **Upload Logo** and select a PNG or SVG file under 2 MB. Your logo appears in the top-left corner of the workspace and in outbound email notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Timezone">
    Choose your workspace timezone from the **Timezone** dropdown. All due dates, scheduled reminders, and activity timestamps display relative to this timezone for every member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save General Settings** to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing & Plan

The Billing section lets you review your active subscription, upgrade your plan, and keep your payment details current without needing to contact support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View Your Current Plan">
    Navigate to **Settings → Billing**. Your active plan, renewal date, and seat count are displayed at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade or Change Your Plan">
    Click **Upgrade Plan** to open the plan comparison view. Select the tier that best fits your team's size and feature requirements, then follow the checkout flow to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Your Billing Email">
    Under **Billing Contact**, enter the email address where invoices and payment receipts should be sent. This can differ from the account owner's email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Payment Method">
    Click **Edit Payment Method** to replace or update your credit card or payment details. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each plan tier enforces limits on the number of seats, active projects, and storage. If you approach a limit, Google displays an in-app banner with upgrade options. Exceeding seat limits prevents new members from joining until you upgrade or remove existing users.
</Note>

## Default Task Settings

Default task settings reduce repetitive configuration by pre-filling common fields every time a new task is created in your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set a Default Assignee">
    Go to **Settings → Tasks → Default Assignee**. Choose a specific team member or select **Unassigned** to leave new tasks unassigned by default. You can always override this on individual tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Default Due Date Offsets">
    Enable **Auto Due Date** and set the offset in days (e.g., `+7` days from creation). Every new task receives an automatic due date calculated from its creation timestamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Default Labels">
    Select one or more labels from the **Default Labels** picker. These labels are applied automatically when a new task is created, making it easier to filter and categorize work from the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Task Defaults">
    Click **Save Task Settings** to apply these defaults workspace-wide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data & Privacy

Google gives you full visibility and control over how your workspace data is stored, retained, and removed.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Data Retention" icon="database" href="#data--privacy">
    By default, completed and archived tasks are retained for 12 months. Adjust the retention window under **Settings → Privacy → Data Retention** to match your organization's compliance requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Export Your Data" icon="arrow-down-to-bracket" href="#data--privacy">
    Download a full export of your workspace — including tasks, comments, attachments, and member activity — from **Settings → Privacy → Export Data**. Exports are delivered as a ZIP file to your billing email within 24 hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deletion Policy" icon="trash" href="#data--privacy">
    Deleted tasks move to the **Trash** and are permanently purged after 30 days. Workspace deletion is irreversible and immediately removes all data, members, and integrations. Contact support before deleting if you need a final export.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audit Logs" icon="list-check" href="#data--privacy">
    Owners and Admins can access a rolling 90-day audit log of all settings changes, member actions, and permission updates under **Settings → Privacy → Audit Log**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Many settings pages have individual **Save** buttons per section. After making changes to any section, scroll to the bottom of that section and click its **Save** button before navigating away — unsaved changes are discarded without a warning prompt.
</Tip>
