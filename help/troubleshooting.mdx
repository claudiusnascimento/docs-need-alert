---

Most issues in Google can be resolved quickly with a few targeted steps. Use the tabs below to find troubleshooting guidance for the category that matches your problem. Work through each step in order — most issues are resolved before you reach the end of the list.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="Login & Access">

    **Can't log in to your account**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm your email address">
        Make sure you're entering the email address you used to sign up. If you have multiple email addresses, try each one on the login page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reset your password">
        Click **Forgot password?** on the login page. Enter your email and follow the link sent to your inbox. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your two-factor authentication (2FA)">
        If you have 2FA enabled, make sure your authenticator app's time is correctly synced. If you've lost access to your 2FA device, use a backup code from when you set up 2FA, or contact [support](/help/contact-support) to verify your identity.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear browser cookies and cache">
        Stale session data can prevent a successful login. Clear your browser's cookies and cache, close the tab, and try logging in again.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ---

    **Account locked or suspended**

    Accounts are temporarily locked after several consecutive failed login attempts as a security measure. Wait 15 minutes and try again. If your account has been suspended for a policy violation, you'll receive an email with details. Reach out to [support](/help/contact-support) if you believe the suspension was made in error.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Notifications">

    **Not receiving email notifications**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your spam or junk folder">
        Email clients sometimes filter automated messages. Search for emails from `notifications@google.app` and mark them as "Not Spam" to ensure future delivery.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your email address">
        Go to **Settings → Profile** and confirm that your email address is correct and verified. If it shows as unverified, click **Resend Verification Email**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review your notification preferences">
        Go to **Settings → Notifications** and check that the notification types you expect are toggled on. You can configure preferences per workspace and per channel (email, in-app, mobile push).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ---

    **Webhook not firing**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm the endpoint URL">
        In **Settings → Integrations → Webhooks**, verify that the destination URL is correct and publicly accessible.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the event triggers">
        Ensure the specific event types (e.g., task created, status changed) are selected for the webhook.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Inspect the delivery log">
        Open the webhook's **Delivery Log** to view recent attempts and HTTP response codes. A `4xx` response indicates an issue with the receiving server; a `5xx` response may be transient — use the **Retry** button to resend failed deliveries.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Integrations">

    **Integration disconnected or showing as inactive**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Re-authorize the integration">
        Go to **Settings → Integrations**, find the affected integration, and click **Reconnect**. You'll be redirected to the third-party service to grant permissions again. This is often required after password changes or permission revocations.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check required permissions">
        Some integrations require specific permission scopes (e.g., read/write access to files or calendars). Review the permissions requested during authorization and make sure you approve all required scopes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Revoke and re-add the integration">
        If reconnecting doesn't resolve the issue, click **Remove** to fully disconnect the integration, then add it again from scratch.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ---

    **Slack integration not posting messages**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Verify the target channel">
        In **Settings → Integrations → Slack**, confirm the channel name is correct and that the Google bot has been invited to the channel. In Slack, type `/invite @Google` in the target channel to add the bot.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check channel permissions">
        Make sure the channel is not archived and that your Slack workspace admin hasn't restricted third-party app posting. Contact your Slack admin if the channel is locked down.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Re-authorize the Slack integration">
        Disconnect and reconnect the Slack integration using the steps above to refresh the OAuth token.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Performance">

    **Slow load times or unresponsive pages**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
        Open your browser settings and clear cached images and files. Reload the Google dashboard and check whether performance improves.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your internet connection">
        Run a speed test at a site like fast.com. If your connection is slow or unstable, that may be the root cause. Try switching networks or restarting your router.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Disable browser extensions">
        Some extensions (ad blockers, script managers) can interfere with web app performance. Open an incognito window (which disables most extensions) and see if Google loads faster.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different browser">
        If the issue persists, test in a different browser to rule out browser-specific problems. Google works best in the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ---

    **Data not syncing or showing stale content**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Force a manual refresh">
        Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (Mac) to perform a hard reload of the page, bypassing the cache.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the API status page">
        Visit the Google status page to see if there is an active incident affecting sync services. If a service disruption is posted, your data will re-sync automatically once the issue is resolved.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign out and sign back in">
        Signing out refreshes your session token and can resolve persistent sync issues. Go to your profile menu and click **Sign Out**, then log in again.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  </Tab>

</Tabs>

<Note>
  If you've worked through the steps above and your issue is still unresolved, the Google support team is ready to help. Visit the [Contact Support](/help/contact-support) page to start a live chat, send an email, or post in the community forum.
</Note>
