Whether you're just getting started or managing a growing team, you're likely to run into questions along the way. Browse the topics below to find quick, clear answers to the most common questions about your Google account, workspace, billing, and data.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    To reset your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page and enter the email address associated with your account. Google will send you a password reset link — check your inbox (and your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes). Click the link, enter a new password, and sign in. If you still can't access your account, contact [support](/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Google for free?">
    Yes. Google offers a free plan that gives you access to core features with limits on workspace members, storage, and integrations. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time to unlock additional capacity, advanced automations, priority support, and more. Visit the **Billing** section in your account settings to review available plans and pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many members can I add to my workspace?">
    The number of members you can invite depends on your plan:

    | Plan       | Member Limit         |
    |------------|----------------------|
    | Free       | Up to 5 members      |
    | Pro        | Up to 50 members     |
    | Enterprise | Unlimited members    |

    To invite members, go to **Settings → Members** and enter their email addresses. Invitations expire after 7 days, so ask teammates to accept promptly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I export my data?">
    You can export your workspace data at any time from **Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data**. Google packages your tasks, projects, comments, and attachments into a downloadable archive (`.zip`). Large exports may take a few minutes to prepare — you'll receive an email with a download link once it's ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Yes. Google encrypts all data in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Your workspace data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers. Google does not sell your data to third parties. You can review our full security practices and compliance certifications in the **Trust Center** linked in the footer of your dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple workspaces?">
    Yes. You can create or join multiple workspaces with a single Google account. Switch between workspaces by clicking your profile avatar in the top-left corner and selecting a workspace from the dropdown. Each workspace has its own members, settings, billing, and data — they are completely isolated from one another.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription** and click **Cancel Plan**. Your paid plan remains active until the end of the current billing period, after which your workspace moves to the free tier. You won't be charged again after cancellation. If you cancel by mistake, you can re-subscribe at any time before the billing period ends.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Your data is retained for 90 days after your subscription ends. During this window you can log in and export your data at any time. After 90 days, your workspace data is permanently deleted from Google's servers in accordance with our data retention policy. If you need a longer retention window, contact [support](/help/contact-support) before cancelling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Google have a mobile app?">
    Yes. Google is available on iOS and Android. Search for **Google** in the App Store or Google Play, install the app, and sign in with your existing account credentials. The mobile app syncs in real time with your web workspace, so you can manage tasks, review notifications, and collaborate with your team from anywhere.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Google offers several support channels depending on your needs. You can start a live chat session from the **Help** menu inside your dashboard (available Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm UTC), send an email to our support team, or post in the community forum. Visit the [Contact Support](/help/contact-support) page for a full overview of response times and the best channel for your issue.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
