## What to Include in Your Request

Providing the right context upfront helps the support team diagnose and resolve your issue faster. Before reaching out, gather the following information:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your workspace name">
    Include the exact name of your workspace (found in **Settings → General**) so the team can locate your account quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A description of the issue">
    Briefly describe what you were trying to do and what happened instead. Clear, specific descriptions reduce back-and-forth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Any error messages">
    Copy the full text of any error messages or alert banners you see. If possible, include a screenshot so the team can see exactly what you're experiencing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Steps to reproduce">
    List the exact steps that lead to the issue — for example: "1. Open a project. 2. Click Add Task. 3. The page goes blank." Reproducible issues are resolved significantly faster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your browser and operating system">
    Specify which browser (e.g., Chrome 124, Safari 17) and operating system (e.g., macOS 14, Windows 11) you're using. This helps narrow down environment-specific bugs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response Times

Response times vary by plan. Enterprise customers also benefit from a dedicated Customer Success Manager for proactive guidance and escalations.

| Plan       | First Response Time | Notes                                      |
|------------|---------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Free       | Within 48 hours     | Email support only                         |
| Pro        | Within 24 hours     | Email and live chat support                |
| Enterprise | Within 4 hours      | Priority routing + dedicated CSM assigned  |

Response times are measured in business hours (Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm UTC) and exclude public holidays.

<Tip>
  Before opening a support ticket, check the [Google Status Page](#) to see if your issue is related to an active service incident. If an incident has been declared, the engineering team is already working on a fix and your data will recover automatically — no ticket needed.
</Tip>
