This quickstart guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to productive in Google. By the end, you'll have a workspace set up, your first task created, and your team invited — all in just a few minutes. No prior experience is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.google.com](https://app.google.com) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**.

    Check your inbox for a verification email from Google and click the **Verify Email** link inside. You must verify your email before you can access your workspace. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend Verification Email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Workspace">
    After verifying your email, you'll be prompted to create your first workspace. Give your workspace a clear, recognizable name — for example, your company or team name.

    You can also upload a workspace logo and choose a primary timezone at this stage. These settings help your teammates identify the workspace and ensure due dates and notifications appear in the correct time context. You can update these details later in **Settings > Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Task">
    Once your workspace is ready, you'll land on the main board view. Click **+ New Task** in the top-right corner of the screen.

    Fill in the following details:

    - **Title** — a short, descriptive name for the task
    - **Description** — any additional context, acceptance criteria, or notes
    - **Due Date** — the date by which the task should be completed
    - **Assignee** — the team member responsible for completing the task
    - **Priority** — choose from Low, Medium, High, or Urgent

    Click **Save Task** when you're done. Your task will appear on the board immediately and will be visible to all workspace members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Bring your teammates into the workspace so you can start collaborating right away. Go to **Settings > Members** using the navigation sidebar.

    Click **Invite Members**, then enter the email addresses of the people you want to add — one per line, or separated by commas. Choose an appropriate role for each member:

    - **Admin** — full access to workspace settings and billing
    - **Member** — can create, edit, and complete tasks
    - **Viewer** — read-only access to the workspace

    Click **Send Invites**. Each invitee will receive an email with a link to join your workspace. They'll need to create their own Google account if they don't have one already.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once your workspace is set up, you can automate repetitive tasks and build custom integrations using the Google REST API. Head over to the [API Introduction](/api/introduction) to generate your first API key and explore available endpoints.
</Note>
