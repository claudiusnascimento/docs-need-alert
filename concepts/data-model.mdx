Every piece of information in Google belongs to a well-defined entity with a clear set of fields and an explicit relationship to its parent. Understanding the data model helps you navigate the platform confidently, design integrations that behave predictably, and make sense of the API responses you work with. The five core entities — Workspace, Project, Task, Comment, and Member — are described below.

## Core Entities

| Entity | Key Fields | Belongs To |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | `id`, `name`, `slug`, `plan` | — (top-level) |
| **Project** | `id`, `name`, `description`, `status` | Workspace |
| **Task** | `id`, `title`, `description`, `status`, `due_date`, `assignee` | Project |
| **Comment** | `id`, `body`, `created_at`, `author` | Task |
| **Member** | `id`, `email`, `role`, `joined_at` | Workspace |

Each entity carries a unique `id` that is stable across its entire lifetime. The `slug` on a Workspace is a URL-safe identifier you choose at creation and use in shareable links. The `plan` field on a Workspace reflects your current subscription tier and determines feature availability across the whole workspace.

## Task Statuses

Tasks move through a defined lifecycle. Each status represents a distinct phase of the work and controls how the task appears in project views, filters, and reports.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **To Do** | The task has been created and is queued for work, but no one has started on it yet. |
| **In Progress** | The assignee is actively working on the task. The task appears in in-progress filters and timeline views. |
| **In Review** | The work is complete from the assignee's perspective and is awaiting review, approval, or QA from another team member. |
| **Done** | The task has been reviewed and accepted. It is closed and moves out of active queues, but remains fully searchable and visible in history. |

You can filter and group your project view by status at any time. Task status changes are recorded in the activity feed with a timestamp and the name of the member who made the change.

## Member Roles

Members are added to a workspace and assigned one of four roles. Each role is a cumulative set of permissions — higher roles include all the capabilities of lower ones plus additional access.

| Role | View Content | Create & Edit | Manage Members | Manage Settings | Billing & Delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Viewer** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Member** | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Admin** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Owner** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

A workspace can have only one Owner at a time. Ownership can be transferred to any existing Admin from the workspace settings page. All other roles can be assigned to as many members as needed.

<Note>
You can export all of your workspace data — including projects, tasks, comments, and member records — at any time from **Workspace Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data**. Exports are delivered as a structured JSON archive to the workspace Owner's email address within a few minutes.
</Note>
