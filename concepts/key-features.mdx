Google is designed to give your team everything it needs to plan, execute, and deliver work — without switching between a dozen different tools. The features below form the foundation of the platform, and each one is built to work alongside the others so that your workflow stays connected from the first task to the final sign-off.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Task Management" icon="list-check">
    Tasks are the core unit of work in Google. You can create a task in seconds, then layer in as much detail as the work requires.

    - **Create and assign** tasks to any workspace member directly from a project view or the global task composer.
    - **Set due dates** to give assignees a clear deadline and surface upcoming work in timeline and calendar views.
    - **Prioritize with labels** by tagging tasks with custom labels — such as *bug*, *feature*, or *urgent* — so your team can filter and sort their queues instantly.
    - **Track status** by moving tasks through configurable stages: To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done.
    - **Subtasks** let you break complex work into smaller, trackable steps without leaving the parent task.

    Every change to a task is recorded in its activity feed, giving you a full audit trail with no extra effort.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Real-Time Collaboration" icon="users">
    Google keeps your whole team on the same page, whether they're in the same room or across time zones.

    - **Comments** on any task let teammates share context, ask questions, and document decisions right where the work lives.
    - **@mentions** notify specific members instantly, pulling the right person into a conversation without a separate message.
    - **Shared views** mean everyone on a project sees the same data in real time — no stale exports, no version conflicts.
    - **Activity feeds** at the task, project, and workspace level show a chronological log of all changes, so you always know what happened and who did it.

    Edits, status changes, and comments all propagate live, so your team never works from outdated information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Smart Notifications" icon="bell">
    Google surfaces the information that matters to you and filters out the noise you don't need.

    - **In-app notifications** appear in your notification center whenever you're @mentioned, assigned to a task, or when a task you're watching changes status.
    - **Email digests** deliver a summary of activity on your tasks and projects on a schedule you choose — immediately, hourly, or daily.
    - **Webhook notifications** push real-time event payloads to any endpoint you configure, making it easy to connect Google to your own systems or automation tools.
    - **Notification preferences** are fully customizable per workspace, so you can tune the signal-to-noise ratio to match how your team works.

    You control what triggers a notification and how it reaches you, keeping your inbox clean and your focus intact.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="API & Webhooks" icon="code">
    Every resource in Google is accessible programmatically through a RESTful API, so you can build exactly the integrations and automations your team needs.

    - **REST API** — create, read, update, and delete workspaces, projects, tasks, members, and comments with standard HTTP requests.
    - **API keys** — generate scoped tokens from your workspace settings to authenticate requests securely without exposing user credentials.
    - **Webhooks** — subscribe to any event (task created, status changed, comment added, and more) and receive a JSON payload at your chosen URL the moment it happens.
    - **Rate limits and pagination** are documented and consistent across all endpoints, making your integrations predictable at scale.

    Use the API to sync data with your other tools, trigger automated workflows from task status changes, or build a fully custom reporting dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Third-Party Integrations" icon="plug">
    Google connects to the tools your team already uses so that your work stays synchronized across your entire stack.

    - **Slack** — receive task updates and @mention notifications in any Slack channel, and create tasks directly from Slack messages.
    - **GitHub** — link pull requests and commits to tasks; task status updates automatically when a linked PR is merged.
    - **Google Calendar** — sync task due dates to your calendar so deadlines always appear alongside your meetings.
    - **Zapier & Make** — trigger thousands of automated workflows without writing code, connecting Google to hundreds of other applications.

    You can enable and configure integrations from the **Integrations** page in your workspace settings. Each integration uses secure OAuth or token-based authentication.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Permissions & Roles" icon="shield-check">
    Google's role system gives you fine-grained control over who can see and do what inside your workspace.

    | Role | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Owner** | Full control over the workspace, including billing, deletion, and all settings. Automatically assigned to the workspace creator. |
    | **Admin** | Can manage members, projects, and integrations. Cannot delete the workspace or change billing. |
    | **Member** | Can create and edit projects and tasks within projects they're assigned to. Cannot manage workspace settings or members. |
    | **Viewer** | Read-only access to projects and tasks they've been granted access to. Cannot create or edit any content. |

    Roles are assigned at the workspace level and apply across all projects by default. Project-level overrides let you grant a Member elevated access to a specific project without changing their workspace-wide role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
The fastest way to extend what Google can do for your team is to explore the **Integrations** page in your workspace settings. Connecting your existing tools — even just Slack and your calendar — can dramatically reduce the time your team spends context-switching.
</Tip>
