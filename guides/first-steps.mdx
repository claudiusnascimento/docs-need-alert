Getting started with Google is straightforward — within a few minutes you can have a fully organized project, a team ready to collaborate, and tasks moving through a clear workflow. This guide walks you through every step from opening your workspace for the first time to watching your team's progress in real time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Your Workspace">
    After signing in, you land on the **Dashboard**. The left sidebar lists all your workspaces and projects. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the layout:

    - **Sidebar** — switch between workspaces, browse projects, and access Settings.
    - **Main panel** — displays your active view (Dashboard, Board, List, or Calendar).
    - **Top bar** — search across your entire workspace, access notifications, and manage your account.

    If you belong to multiple workspaces, use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar to select the correct one before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a New Project">
    A project is the top-level container for all your tasks and team activity.

    1. Click **New Project** in the sidebar (or press `N` from any screen).
    2. Enter a **Project Name** that clearly identifies the initiative (for example, "Q3 Marketing Campaign").
    3. Add an optional **Description** to give teammates context about the project's goals.
    4. Choose a **Color** to make the project easy to spot in the sidebar and on reports.
    5. Select a **Visibility** level — *Private* (invite-only) or *Team* (visible to everyone in your workspace).
    6. Click **Create Project** to save.

    Your new project opens immediately in the default List view.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Tasks to the Project">
    Tasks are the individual units of work inside a project. Add your first task to get the board moving.

    1. Click **+ Add Task** at the bottom of any list section, or press `T`.
    2. Enter a clear, action-oriented **Title** (for example, "Draft homepage copy").
    3. Set a **Due Date** using the date picker so the task appears on the Calendar view.
    4. Optionally add a **Description**, attach files, or add labels to help teammates understand the scope.
    5. Press **Enter** or click **Save** to create the task.

    Repeat this process to build out your task list. You can always bulk-import tasks from a CSV later under **Settings > Import**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign Team Members">
    Bringing your team into the project ensures everyone has visibility and accountability.

    1. Open the project and click the **Settings** (gear) icon near the project name.
    2. Navigate to the **Members** tab.
    3. Type a teammate's name or email address in the **Invite Members** field and press **Enter**.
    4. Select a **Role** for each member:
       - **Viewer** — can read tasks and comments but cannot edit.
       - **Editor** — can create, edit, and complete tasks.
       - **Admin** — has full control including project settings and member management.
    5. Click **Send Invites**. Teammates receive an email notification and can join immediately.

    To assign a specific task to a member, open the task and click the **Assignee** field, then select the person's name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Progress">
    Once tasks are created and assigned, use Google's views to monitor the state of your work at a glance.

    - **Board view** — drag and drop tasks between status columns (To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done) for a Kanban-style overview.
    - **List view** — see all tasks in a structured list with inline editing for due dates, assignees, and priorities.
    - **Calendar view** — visualize tasks by due date to spot scheduling conflicts early.

    Switch between views using the view selector in the top-left corner of the project. Filter by assignee, label, or due date to focus on exactly what matters to you right now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Speed up repetitive actions with keyboard shortcuts. Press **`?`** anywhere in the app to open the full shortcuts reference. Common shortcuts include `N` to create a project, `T` to add a task, `B` to switch to Board view, and `L` to switch to List view.
</Tip>
