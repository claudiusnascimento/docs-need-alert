Staying informed is important, but notification overload is a real productivity killer. Google gives you fine-grained control over which events trigger a notification, which channel delivers it, and how often you hear from us. Spend a few minutes configuring your preferences now and you will receive exactly the information you need — nothing more.

## Notification Types

The table below lists every notification event Google can generate and the delivery channels each one supports.

| Event | In-App | Email | Webhook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task assigned to you | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Task due soon (24 hrs) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Task overdue | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Comment added to your task | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| @mention in a comment | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Task status changed | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| New member added to project | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Project report ready | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

> **Key:** ✅ Supported &nbsp;&nbsp; ❌ Not available for this channel

## Configuring In-App Notifications

In-app notifications appear as a numbered badge on the bell icon in the top-right corner of every page. Click the bell to open the notification center.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner.
    2. Select **Notification Preferences** from the dropdown menu.
    3. The preferences panel opens with separate sections for *In-App*, *Email*, and *Webhooks*.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Which Events You Want In-App">
    1. Under the **In-App** section, you see a toggle for every supported event.
    2. Turn off events that are not relevant to your role. For example, if you are not a project admin, you may want to disable *New Member Added* notifications.
    3. Toggle **All In-App Notifications** at the top of the section to enable or disable the channel entirely while preserving your individual settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Your Preferences">
    1. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel.
    2. Your preferences take effect immediately — you will not receive in-app notifications for events you have disabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email Notifications

Email notifications are batched according to a delivery frequency you choose, so you can stay informed without your inbox filling up every few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Your Email Digest Frequency">
    1. In **Notification Preferences**, click the **Email** tab.
    2. Use the **Digest Frequency** dropdown to select one of four options:

       | Option | Behavior |
       |---|---|
       | **Instant** | Sends a separate email for each qualifying event as it happens. |
       | **Daily** | Bundles all qualifying events into a single daily summary email, sent at a time you choose. |
       | **Weekly** | Sends one summary email at the start of your chosen day each week. |
       | **Off** | Disables all email notifications. In-app and webhook alerts are unaffected. |

    3. If you choose *Daily* or *Weekly*, use the **Send At** field to set the preferred delivery time and day.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Which Events Trigger Email">
    1. Below the frequency selector, toggle individual events on or off to fine-tune what appears in your digest.
    2. Events not available for email delivery (see the table above) are grayed out.
    3. Click **Save Changes** when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Email notifications are sent from **notifications@google.app**. Add this address to your contacts or allowlist to prevent digest emails from landing in spam.
</Info>

## Webhooks

Webhooks let you push notification events to any external URL — a custom Slack bot, a logging service, a CI/CD pipeline, or any system that can receive an HTTP POST request.

To set up a webhook endpoint, visit the [Webhooks reference](/api/webhooks) page, which covers payload schemas, authentication headers, retry behavior, and example integration code.

Once your endpoint is ready, register it under **Settings > Integrations > Webhooks** by entering the URL and selecting the events you want to receive. Google sends a verification ping to the URL and begins delivering payloads immediately after confirmation.

<Tip>
  Use **Do Not Disturb** mode to silence all in-app notifications during focus time or outside working hours. Enable it by clicking your profile avatar and selecting **Do Not Disturb**, then choose a duration (1 hour, 4 hours, until tomorrow, or a custom end time). Webhook deliveries continue uninterrupted during Do Not Disturb — only the in-app badge and alert sounds are suppressed.
</Tip>
