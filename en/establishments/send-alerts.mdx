When the item becomes available, you send an alert and everyone in the queue is notified at the same time.

<Info>
  Sending alerts requires the **Dispatch events** permission. The owner can always send.
</Info>

## Send now

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item and click Send alert">
    Need Alert calculates the cost on the spot and shows how many people will be notified, how many credits the dispatch uses, and your current balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the alert">
    - **Title (optional):** if left blank, the title is the item name.
    - **Message:** up to 200 characters. Example: "A fresh batch just came out of the oven, come get it while it's warm!"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Send**. The status shows **Sending** and changes to **Sent** once everyone has been notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to the queue

- In a **single queue**, each person notified leaves the queue.
- In a **recurring queue**, the person stays in the queue, unless they reached the number of alerts they chose.

People get a notification on their phone or computer, if they allowed notifications, and the alert also appears under **Notifications** inside Need Alert.

## Cost

Each person notified uses **1 credit**. The charge happens at dispatch time, based on who is in the queue at that moment.

- **Empty queue:** the dispatch is recorded and costs nothing.
- **Insufficient balance:** the dispatch isn't sent. The dialog shows how much is missing and, if you have the **Manage billing** permission, a **Buy credits** link. See [Credits](/en/establishments/credits).

## When a dispatch isn't accepted

| Situation | Reason |
|---|---|
| The item is paused or archived | Only **Active** items can receive dispatches. |
| The unit is inactive or suspended | A suspended unit can't send new alerts. |
| A dispatch for this item is already in progress | Wait for the previous one to finish. |
| You just sent an alert for this item | There's a short gap of a few seconds between dispatches of the same item, to prevent double clicks. |

## History

The **Dispatches** section lists every alert from the unit: date, item, message, status, recipients, credits used, how many notifications were delivered and how many failed, and who sent it. Each item's latest dispatches also appear on the item page.

<Tip>
  Want to notify people at a set time, like when the oven is done at 4 p.m.? [Schedule the dispatch](/en/establishments/schedule-alerts).
</Tip>
