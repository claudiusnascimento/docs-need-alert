You don't have to do everything yourself. Invite the people you work with and choose what each of them can do at each unit.

## Permissions

Permissions are independent and can be combined freely.

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **View dashboard** | See items, subscribers, and dispatches. Every member has it. |
| **Create items** | Add new items. |
| **Edit items** | Edit, pause, archive, and change the picture of items. |
| **Dispatch events** | Send, schedule, reschedule, and cancel alerts. Also receives the balance warnings for scheduled dispatches. |
| **Manage billing** | See the balance and statement, and buy credits. |

The **owner** always has every permission, and is the only one who edits the unit's **Settings**, invites and removes people, manages roles, and edits the [chain catalog](/en/establishments/chain-catalog).

Permissions apply **per unit**. The same person can send alerts at one shop and only view another.

## Roles

A **role** is a named set of permissions, like "Cashier" or "Stock manager". Changing a role's permissions changes access for everyone with that role at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles and click Create role">
    Give it a name and check the permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default role, if you want">
    Check **Default role for new members** so it's preselected in invites.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A role that any member has can't be deleted. Change those members' role first.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team and click Invite">
    Enter the person's email and role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The person gets the invite by email">
    They sign in with their existing account or create one on the spot. If they already have an account, the invite also shows in their menu under **Pending invites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The person accepts">
    Once they click **Accept invite**, they join the team. Nobody is added without accepting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The invite is tied to the invited email. If the person is signed in with a different email, they need to sign out and sign in with the one that got the invite. Invites expire after 7 days.
</Note>

Until the invite is accepted, it shows under **Invites** on the same page. You can **Resend** or **Cancel invite**. Once cancelled, the link in the email stops working.

## Change a member's role or remove them

Under **Members**, use each person's actions menu to change their role or **Remove** them. A removed member loses access to that unit's dashboard immediately.
