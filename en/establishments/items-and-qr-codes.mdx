An **item** is what people wait for at your unit: a product, a dish, a slot, a class. Each item has its own subscribers, its own QR code, and a public link.

<Info>
  Creating items requires the **Create items** permission. Editing, archiving, and changing the picture require **Edit items**. The owner has all permissions.
</Info>

## Create an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Items and click New item">
    If your chain has a [catalog](/en/establishments/chain-catalog), pick a product under **From the catalog** to fill in the name, description, and picture. You can adjust everything before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the content">
    **Name** and **Description** are what customers see. You can fill them in Portuguese and English: each person sees the item in their language and, if a translation is missing, in the chain's default language, which is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the alert type">
    - **One-time alert:** the customer is notified once and the subscription closes.
    - **Recurring alert:** the subscription stays active and the customer can be notified many times.

    See the difference in [How it works](/en/how-it-works).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save item**. The QR code is generated right away and **never changes**, so you can print it with confidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sensitive data

Check **Requires consent for sensitive data** when subscribing reveals health data, such as a blood type or a medical specialty. People subscribing must confirm an extra consent, and the public page says so up front.

## QR code and poster

On the item page, the **QR Code** card points to the item's public link. You can:

- **Download SVG**, for print shops and large materials.
- **Download PNG**, for social media and messages.
- **Print poster**, a ready-made page with the item name, the QR code, and the instruction "point your phone's camera".
- **Copy link**, to share by WhatsApp or email, or to add to your website.

The item's **Picture** shows on the public page and on the poster. Use JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 5 MB.

## Follow your subscribers

The **Subscribers** column shows how many people are waiting for each item, updated in real time. On the item page, the **Subscribers** card lists who is waiting and since when. For privacy, only first names are shown.

## Item status

| Status | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Accepts new subscriptions and can receive dispatches. |
| **Paused** | The public page says the item is paused. Nobody can subscribe and you can't send alerts. |
| **Archived** | The item is retired: subscriptions are closed, scheduled dispatches are cancelled, and the QR code shows the item is no longer available. **This can't be undone.** |

To archive, use **Archive** in the list or on the item page.

<Warning>
  Changing the alert type of an item that already has people waiting changes what happens to them on the next alert. The form warns you before saving.
</Warning>
