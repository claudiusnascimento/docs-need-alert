**Need Alert** is an **on-demand waitlist and alert** platform. It takes the "notify me when it's back" button from online stores to any business: the bakery taking bread out of the oven, the clinic opening a last-minute slot, the blood bank that needs a specific blood type.

Here's how it works: your customer joins an item's queue through a QR code or a link. When the item becomes available, you send an alert and everyone in the queue is notified on their phone at the same time.

## Why use it

- **Works without a website.** A QR code on the shop window or the shelf is already a waitlist.
- **No app to install.** Your customers use Need Alert in the browser and can add it to their home screen if they want.
- **You see the demand.** The dashboard shows how many people are waiting for each item, in real time.
- **Predictable cost.** Before each dispatch you see how many people will be notified and how many credits it uses.
