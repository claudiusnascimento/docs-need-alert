You don't need to install anything. Need Alert works in your phone's or computer's browser.

## With a QR code or link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Point your phone's camera at the establishment's QR code, or open the link they shared. The item page shows the establishment and how many people are waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Sign in to join the queue**. If you don't have an account yet, sign up with your name, email, and password. After signing in you go straight back to the item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the queue">
    Click **Join the queue**, choose how long you want to be notified, and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on notifications">
    Need Alert asks for permission to send notifications. Click **Enable alerts** and allow it in the browser. Without this permission, the alert only shows while Need Alert is open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  On iPhone and iPad, notifications only arrive when Need Alert is installed on the home screen. See [Alerts and the app](/en/customers/alerts-and-app).
</Warning>

## Choose how long to be notified

| Option | When it shows | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Until when** | In every queue | No end date, for 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, or until a date you choose. |
| **How many times to notify** | Only in recurring queues | Every time it happens, or a set number of times. |

In a **single queue**, you're notified once and your subscription closes. To be notified again, join the queue again.

You can change these choices later in [My queues](/en/customers/my-queues).

## Queues with sensitive data

Some queues reveal health data, like your blood type in a donation campaign. In those cases, the page tells you up front, and you need to check an extra consent to join.

## Find establishments without a QR code

In **Find establishments**, search by name, neighborhood, or city, filter by category, or use **Near me** with the radius you prefer. On the establishment's page, **Open queues** lists the available items. Click **See queue and join**.

## When you can't join

- **Paused item:** the establishment paused the queue for now. Come back later.
- **Item no longer available:** the establishment closed that item's queue.
