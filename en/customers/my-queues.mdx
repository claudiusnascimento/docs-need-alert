**My queues** brings together all your subscriptions, at any establishment.

## Active and closed

- **Active:** the queues you are waiting in right now.
- **Closed:** subscriptions that have ended, for the reason shown in the **Status** column.

For each subscription you see the item, the establishment, how many alerts you've received, the end date, and when you were last notified.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In queue** | You're waiting and will be notified on the next dispatch. |
| **Fulfilled** | You got the alerts you asked for. |
| **Expired** | The end date you chose has passed. |
| **You left** | You left the queue. |
| **Cancelled** | The establishment closed that item's queue. |

## Edit or leave

From each subscription's actions menu:

- **Open item:** go to the item page.
- **Edit:** change how long, and how many times, you want to be notified.
- **Leave the queue:** you stop getting alerts for that item. To come back, just join the queue again.

<Tip>
  Installed on your home screen, Need Alert opens straight to **My queues** and shows the data from your last visit even when you're offline.
</Tip>
