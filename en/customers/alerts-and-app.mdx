## How alerts arrive

When the establishment sends the alert, you get:

- a **notification** on your phone or computer, even with the browser closed, if you allowed notifications;
- the alert under **Notifications**, the bell at the top of Need Alert.

Everyone in the queue is notified at the same time.

## Turn on notifications

When you join your first queue, Need Alert asks whether you want to be notified. Click **Enable alerts** and allow it in the browser.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Notifications are blocked">
    If you declined the permission before, the browser won't ask again. Open the site settings in your browser, usually through the lock next to the address, and allow notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I use an iPhone or iPad">
    On iPhone and iPad, notifications only arrive when Need Alert is installed on the home screen. Install it with the steps below and turn on notifications from the installed app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Add to your home screen

Once installed, Need Alert opens like an app, with no address bar, straight to **My queues**. No app store needed.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Android and desktop">
    Open **Install app** in the Need Alert menu and click **Install**.

    If the button doesn't show up, open the browser menu and look for **Install app** or **Add to home screen**. If the option is missing, this browser doesn't install web apps.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPhone and iPad">
    1. Open Need Alert in Safari.
    2. Tap **Share** in the browser bar.
    3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
    4. Tap **Add** to confirm.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Language, time zone, and appearance

In **My account** → **Preferences** you choose:

- **Language:** Portuguese (Brazil) or English. Need Alert's interface and emails follow your choice. The alert message itself is written by the establishment.
- **Time zone:** used to show dates and times in your local time.
- **Appearance:** light, dark, or your system's. It applies only to the device you're using.
