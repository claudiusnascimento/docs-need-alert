"Waiting for something" is a problem in every industry. Here are a few examples.

## Brick-and-mortar shops

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bakery" icon="bread-slice">
    QR code on the window: "cheese bread is out of the oven". Everyone who scanned it is notified when the batch is ready. Use a **single queue**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Neighborhood store" icon="shop">
    A QR code on the shelf of an out-of-stock product. You notify people when you restock.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ice cream shop" icon="ice-cream">
    One item per flavor: "let me know when you make it again".
  </Card>
  <Card title="Food truck" icon="truck">
    A fixed QR code at your spot. You [schedule](/en/establishments/schedule-alerts) the alert for when you arrive.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Restaurants

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Buffet" icon="utensils">
    One item per dish of the day. You see how many people are waiting for the stroganoff and decide whether to make more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Seasonal dish" icon="fish">
    Customers are notified the day the dish is back on the menu.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Healthcare

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Clinics and practices" icon="stethoscope">
    A last-minute slot queue: when a patient cancels, everyone in the queue is notified and the first to confirm gets the slot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Blood bank" icon="droplet">
    One item per blood type, with a **recurring queue**: donors sign up once and are called whenever their type runs low.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  When joining a queue reveals health data (blood type, medical specialty), mark the item as **Requires consent for sensitive data**. People confirm an extra consent when joining. See [Items and QR codes](/en/establishments/items-and-qr-codes).
</Note>

## Services with limited spots

- **Courses and gyms:** a waitlist for a full class, with an alert when someone drops out.
- **Hair salons and barbershops:** an alert when a slot opens up after a cancellation.
- **Car repair shops:** "your car is ready for pickup".
- **Equipment rental:** an alert when a rented piece of equipment is returned.

## Automatic integrations

With the [API](/en/api/overview), your own system sends the alert:

- Your **ERP** notifies people when a SKU is back in stock.
- Your **clinic's booking system** notifies people when an appointment is cancelled.
- Your **delivery platform** notifies people when a restaurant starts taking orders again.
