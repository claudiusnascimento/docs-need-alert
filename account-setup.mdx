Your Google account is the foundation of everything you do on the platform. Taking a few minutes to configure your profile, tighten up your security settings, and connect the apps you already use will make your day-to-day experience smoother and more personalized. This page walks you through each area of account configuration in detail.

## Profile Settings

Your profile is visible to everyone in your workspace, so keeping it accurate helps teammates identify and collaborate with you more easily.

To update your profile, go to **Settings > Profile** in the navigation sidebar. From there you can configure the following fields.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Display Name">
    Enter the name you'd like teammates to see when you comment on tasks, send messages, or appear in assignee lists. This does not have to match the name on your account registration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Profile Avatar">
    Upload a photo or image to use as your avatar. Supported formats are JPEG, PNG, and WebP, with a maximum file size of 5 MB. Your avatar appears next to your name throughout the workspace. If you don't upload one, Google uses your initials as a default avatar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Timezone">
    Select your local timezone from the dropdown menu. Google uses this setting to display due dates, scheduled notifications, and activity timestamps in your local time. If you travel frequently, update this setting whenever you change regions to keep your calendar and reminders accurate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Language">
    Choose your preferred display language from the list of supported locales. Changing this setting will reload the interface in the selected language immediately. Workspace content created by other members — such as task titles and comments — is not translated automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the Profile page to apply any updates you make.

## Security

Protecting your account is critical, especially in shared workspaces. Google offers password management and two-factor authentication (2FA) to keep your account secure.

### Change Your Password

To update your password, navigate to **Settings > Security** and click **Change Password**. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Your new password must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Click **Update Password** to save the change.

### Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of protection to your account. Even if someone obtains your password, they won't be able to sign in without the second factor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Security** and locate the **Two-Factor Authentication** section. Click **Enable 2FA** to begin the setup process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an Authentication Method">
    Select your preferred 2FA method:

    - **Authenticator App** — use an app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password to generate time-based one-time passwords (TOTP)
    - **SMS** — receive a one-time code via text message each time you sign in

    For stronger security, the authenticator app method is recommended over SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR Code or Enter the Setup Key">
    If you chose the authenticator app method, open your authentication app and scan the QR code displayed on screen. If your app doesn't support QR scanning, click **Show Setup Key** and enter the code manually into your app.

    For SMS, enter your mobile phone number (including country code) and click **Send Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Activate">
    Enter the six-digit code generated by your authenticator app (or sent to your phone) into the **Verification Code** field and click **Verify**. Once confirmed, 2FA will be active on your account immediately.

    Copy the backup recovery codes displayed on the next screen and store them somewhere safe. You can use these codes to access your account if you ever lose access to your second factor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable two-factor authentication as soon as you set up your account. It only takes two minutes, and it dramatically reduces the risk of unauthorized access — especially if you use Google on shared or public devices.
</Tip>

## Connected Apps

Google integrates with a range of third-party services to streamline your workflow. Connecting your external accounts lets you sync calendars, import files, and receive notifications in the tools you already use.

To manage connected apps, go to **Settings > Connected Apps**. You'll see a list of available integrations organized by category.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Connecting an App">
    Find the app you want to connect in the list and click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to the third-party service to authorize access. Review the requested permissions, then click **Allow** or **Authorize** to complete the connection. You'll be returned to Google automatically once the authorization is complete.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Disconnecting an App">
    To remove a connected app, click the **···** menu next to the integration and select **Disconnect**. Disconnecting an app immediately revokes Google's access to that service. Any data already synced — such as imported calendar events — will remain in your workspace unless you delete it manually.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Managing Permissions">
    Some integrations allow you to adjust the scope of access granted to Google. Click **Manage Permissions** next to a connected app to review and modify what data Google can read or write on your behalf. Changes take effect immediately without requiring you to reconnect the app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  The availability of specific integrations depends on your workspace plan. If an integration you need is not listed, contact your workspace admin or check the [Integrations section](/guides/integrations) of the documentation for details on your plan's supported connections.
</Info>
